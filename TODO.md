# Pace Arena delivery outcomes

## Identity, accessibility, responsive layout, and navigation — in progress
- [ ] Deliver a professional, original Pace Arena brand for peer-to-peer football esports challenges, initial market Kenya, future-expansion ready; support eFootball/PES and EA Sports FC/FIFA across mobile, PC, and gaming consoles without copyrighted publisher logos or implied affiliation.
- [ ] Use a dark premium esports interface, near-black/charcoal backgrounds, bright green accent for active/primary actions, white primary text, muted secondary text, subtle gradients, clean cards, restrained shadows, consistent spacing, football-inspired visuals, clear type hierarchy, and a competitive trustworthy appearance.
- [ ] Make the product responsive and usable on mobile browsers, tablets, laptops, and desktops; use desktop sidebar navigation where appropriate, mobile bottom/compact navigation, and consistent accessible controls, badges, dialogs, and notifications.
- [ ] Provide loading, empty, success, validation and error states; confirmation for important actions; keyboard access, semantic HTML, accessible names, input-linked errors, accessible dialogs, readable timers and match codes, mobile-friendly tables, no horizontal overflow, and status communication that is not color-only. Honor reduced-motion preferences.
- [ ] Use actual application data for online, availability, challenges and matches; never label fictitious records as live. No UI action may be a dead button.

## Authentication, profiles, and authorization — in progress
- [ ] Support email/password registration and login without requiring email verification or sending verification emails. Create the user, profile and authenticated session so a new account can enter immediately; do not create a demo wallet or issue a demo-credit starter grant. Existing accounts with no `emailVerifiedAt` must still be able to log in. Preserve single-use password-reset links, logout, persistent sessions, protected routes, private-by-default loading/redirect behavior, clear validation and error messages; SMTP delivery is only required for password reset.
- [ ] Let a player edit a profile with unique username, avatar, preferred football game, game edition, gaming platform, supported multiplayer compatibility, country and optional general location, online availability, match preferences, account status and eligibility status. Minimize collected personal data and do not unnecessarily expose email, private contact details, precise location, or account identifiers.
- [ ] Enforce Player and Administrator roles server-side. Players may manage their own profile, see eligible KES challenges when enabled, use their KES wallet/ledger when enabled, use authorized match rooms, submit evidence, report disputes, and see history/notifications. Admins may review activity/reported matches/conflicting evidence and pending KES withdrawals; resolve disputes with documented reasons; suspend/reinstate accounts; inspect legacy demo ledger records as historical records; review KES transactions; view audit logs; and manage platform configuration only subject to trusted authorization. Preserve existing account roles; only an existing administrator may change another user's role through an authenticated server action that requires a reason of at least 10 characters, records actor/target/old role/new role/reason in the audit log, and prevents removal of the final administrator. Neither an email allowlist nor a user-editable field/request may grant admin.

Show agreed KES terms to both participants before play.

Keep direct/private invitations visible only to their creator and selected invitee; after a public challenge is accepted, expired, cancelled, or otherwise closed, deny detail access to nonparticipants.

## KES wallet, partner-issued credits, and manual withdrawals — in progress
- [ ] Keep KES funds separate from historic demo credits. Store integer whole-shilling balances, available/reserved amounts, partner-event records and an append-only idempotent ledger; never use floating-point math or accept client-submitted resulting balances. Give each user a stable random wallet reference. The authenticated KES wallet page may link to the external partner purchase site only when `PACE_CREDIT_PARTNER_URL` is configured; Pace Arena does not initiate checkout or STK.
- [ ] Credit a user's KES wallet only from a server-to-server event signed by the partner site after its purchase is settled. Define `POST /api/partner/v1/credit-events` with `X-Pace-Timestamp` (Unix seconds) and `X-Pace-Signature: sha256=<hex>`, HMAC-SHA256 over `<timestamp>.<exact raw request body>` using a server-only shared secret. Validate timestamp within five minutes, constant-time signature comparison, `currency: KES`, positive integer `amountKes`, wallet reference, event type and unique partner event/transaction IDs. Persist valid events and the corresponding KES ledger mutation atomically; duplicate deliveries are idempotent. Never trust browser redirects, user-entered values, unsigned callbacks, or partner claims without a valid signature. Reject invalid requests without exposing the secret.
- [ ] Support signed `credit` and `reversal` events. Reversal must be idempotent and audited; if credited funds have already been reserved or spent, flag/freeze the account for admin review rather than creating an unexplained negative wallet. When signing configuration or wallet settings are missing, fail closed with a clear not-configured state and never simulate funds.
- [ ] Let a player request a manual withdrawal by entering a KES amount and Kenyan M-Pesa phone number. Normalize/validate the phone server-side; validate configured limits; atomically reserve the amount from available KES so it cannot be double-spent; create a pending request and notify administrators. A request is not a payout and must not be shown as paid until reconciled by an admin.
- [ ] Provide an administrator-only manual withdrawal queue. The administrator sends money outside Pace Arena using the approved business account; the application must never initiate a payout transfer. Mark a request paid only after the admin records the external M-Pesa receipt/reference, actual amount and a reason; make settlement atomic/idempotent and audit actor, request, phone, amount, receipt/reference and time. Reject a request only with a reason and release the reserved KES atomically; record the rejection in the ledger/audit trail and notify the player. Prevent duplicate payout completion or editing a paid request.
- [ ] Use KES as the only currency for new match stakes and settlement. For a KES challenge, reserve the agreed KES stakes from both players; settle winner reward, draw refund, cancellation/dispute refund, and upheld dispute only in KES; record linked ledger and audit entries atomically/idempotently. Require `PACE_REAL_WAGERING_ENABLED=true` for KES challenge creation/acceptance/settlement; default this flag off and make it server-only. KES credits also remain unavailable unless `PACE_KES_WALLET_ENABLED=true`, the partner HMAC secret is configured, and any required limits are present. Do not expose feature switches in the player UI or use real money in tests.

## Evidence, five-minute deadline, and settlement — in progress
- [ ] On match end collect each player's final-score screenshot, reported player/home score, opponent score and optional comments. Validate file type and size, participation, deadline, duplicate submissions, private storage authorization and match ownership. Persist upload timestamp and evidence metadata; screenshots are evidence, not infallible or independently authenticated proof.
- [ ] When entering AWAITING_EVIDENCE set server-generated deadline exactly five minutes after the evidence window begins, notify both, permit authorized submissions through the deadline and record server submission times. Process timeouts server-side, not via an open browser timer.
- [ ] A confirmed KES score result may settle only after both participant reports match and all configured review rules pass; a draw refunds each player's own KES stake. A cancellation releases both KES reservations. Existing in-progress demo matches may settle only under their original demo terms and historic demo ledger. A settlement validates state and participants, is atomic/idempotent and records immutable audit information.
- [ ] One missing submission preserves the other's valid evidence, enters timeout review and permits forfeit only when published rules/evidence support it, with reason recorded. Neither submits: do not award an automatic win; review or cancel/refund under documented rules. Conflicting screenshots freeze settlement, enter UNDER_REVIEW and notify participants; never blindly transfer credits or KES on deadline.
