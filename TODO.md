# Pace Arena delivery outcomes

## Identity, accessibility, responsive layout, and navigation — in progress
- [ ] Deliver a professional, original Pace Arena brand for peer-to-peer football esports challenges, initial market Kenya, future-expansion ready; support eFootball/PES and EA Sports FC/FIFA across mobile, PC, and gaming consoles without copyrighted publisher logos or implied affiliation.
- [ ] Use a dark premium esports interface, near-black/charcoal backgrounds, bright green accent for active/primary actions, white primary text, muted secondary text, subtle gradients, clean cards, restrained shadows, consistent spacing, football-inspired visuals, clear type hierarchy, and a competitive trustworthy appearance.
- [ ] Make the product responsive and usable on mobile browsers, tablets, laptops, and desktops; use desktop sidebar navigation where appropriate, mobile bottom/compact navigation, and consistent accessible controls, badges, dialogs, and notifications.
- [ ] Provide loading, empty, success, validation and error states; confirmation for important actions; keyboard access, semantic HTML, accessible names, input-linked errors, accessible dialogs, readable timers and match codes, mobile-friendly tables, no horizontal overflow, and status communication that is not color-only. Honor reduced-motion preferences.
- [ ] Use actual application data for online, availability, challenges and matches; never label fictitious records as live. No UI action may be a dead button.

## Authentication, profiles, and authorization — in progress
- [ ] Support email/password registration and login without requiring email verification or sending verification emails. Create the user, profile, wallet, one-time starter grant and authenticated session so a new account can enter immediately; existing accounts with no `emailVerifiedAt` must also be able to log in. Preserve single-use password-reset links, logout, persistent sessions, protected routes, private-by-default loading/redirect behavior, clear validation and error messages; SMTP delivery is only required for password reset.
- [ ] Let a player edit a profile with unique username, avatar, preferred football game, game edition, gaming platform, supported multiplayer compatibility, country and optional general location, online availability, match preferences, account status and eligibility status. Minimize collected personal data and do not unnecessarily expose email, private contact details, precise location, or account identifiers.
- [ ] Enforce Player and Administrator roles server-side. Players may manage their own profile, see eligible challenges, create/accept challenges, access their own wallet/ledger, use authorized match rooms, submit evidence, report disputes, and see history/notifications. Admins may review activity/reported matches/conflicting evidence, resolve disputes with documented reasons, suspend/reinstate accounts, review demo transactions, view audit logs, and manage platform configuration only subject to trusted authorization. Preserve existing roles; only an existing administrator may change another user's role through an authenticated server action that requires a reason of at least 10 characters, records actor/target/old role/new role/reason in the audit log, and prevents removal of the final administrator. Neither an email allowlist nor a user-editable field/request may grant admin.

## Dashboard, listings, and compatibility — in progress
- [ ] Show the player's demo-credit balance; available opponents; open challenges; incoming invitations; active matches; matches awaiting evidence; recent match history; persisted notifications; and a quick create-challenge action.
- [ ] Filter listings by football game, game edition, gaming platform, compatible cross-play group, demo-credit stake amount and availability. Respect actual edition, device compatibility, online multiplayer requirements and cross-play restrictions; do not assume all mobile/PC/PlayStation/Xbox combinations can play together.
- [ ] Display accurate match/challenge statuses and status labels based on application data.

## Challenges and matchmaking — in progress
- [ ] Create challenges with selected football game, edition, platform, compatible opponent requirements, demo-credit stake, match duration/agreed settings, optional description, an optional direct invitation by username, and invitation expiration. A direct invitation is visible only to its creator and its selected, currently eligible, available and exactly compatible player; only that intended recipient may accept or decline it. A decline returns the creator's reserved stake, changes the challenge to a terminal rejected state and notifies the creator. Prefill the creator's game, exact edition and platform from the saved profile; explain when the profile is incomplete, unavailable, ineligible or mismatched, link to profile settings, and disable publishing until the server-required profile terms match.
- [ ] Allow one eligible opponent only. Prevent self-acceptance, simultaneous double acceptance, incompatible participants, ineligible/suspended accounts and stakes outside server-validated minimum/maximum. Verify balance; reserve both stakes safely/atomically; handle retry/idempotency; expire abandoned invitations and notify the invitee and creator; prevent creating a challenge while the creator is in an active match. Show agreed terms to both participants before play.

## Demo wallet and ledger — in progress
- [ ] Distinguish available demo credits, reserved demo credits, and completed demo-credit transactions; show wallet and transaction history to the owning player.
- [ ] Persist transaction ID, user, optional match, type, amount, prior/resulting balance or equivalent auditable ledger entries, status, server timestamp, and idempotency reference.
- [ ] Support a one-time 1,000-demo-credit grant atomically when an account is created, including legacy accounts that were created before signup email delivery failed and therefore lack a wallet. Record the grant in the ledger and make it idempotent per account; reject duplicate email addresses and usernames; do not imply separate-email account recreation is Sybil-proof. Also support stake reservation/release, winner reward, draw refund, cancellation refund, and documented-reason admin adjustment. Every balance mutation must be atomic, auditable, idempotent, and linked to a ledger record. Clients may never edit balances or submit authoritative resulting balances; prevent concurrent overspending and duplicate starter-credit claims where practical.
- [ ] Credits have no monetary value and cannot be bought, withdrawn or redeemed. Never implement real-money wagering, deposits, withdrawals, cash prizes, payment processing or cash payouts. Disclose any demo fee before acceptance; otherwise apply no fee. Document starter-credit policy.

## Match room, readiness, and lifecycle — in progress
- [ ] After acceptance and successful reservation of both demo stakes, create a private match room displaying usernames/avatars, game/edition/platforms, agreed settings, each player's readiness, private in-game codes, instructions, countdown/elapsed time, current status, evidence deadline and report-problem action.
- [ ] Restrict codes to authorized participants; never show them in public listings. Require both participants to confirm readiness; start timer only after backend validation when both are ready. Handle non-readiness with configured timeout/cancellation rules. Derive countdowns from server timestamps, browser is display only.
- [ ] Implement explicit validated states DRAFT, OPEN, ACCEPTED, AWAITING_STAKES, READY, IN_PROGRESS, AWAITING_EVIDENCE, UNDER_REVIEW, COMPLETED, DRAWN, CANCELLED, REFUNDED. Do not permit arbitrary frontend state changes. Record creation/acceptance/reservation/readiness/start/expected end/reported end/evidence deadline/settlement times and cancellation/dispute reasons. Enforce trusted server timeouts.

## Evidence, five-minute deadline, and settlement — in progress
- [ ] On match end collect each player's final-score screenshot, reported player/home score, opponent score and optional comments. Validate file type and size, participation, deadline, duplicate submissions, private storage authorization and match ownership. Persist upload timestamp and evidence metadata; screenshots are evidence, not infallible or independently authenticated proof.
- [ ] When entering AWAITING_EVIDENCE set server-generated deadline exactly five minutes after the evidence window begins, notify both, permit authorized submissions through the deadline and record server submission times. Process timeouts server-side, not via an open browser timer.
- [ ] Compatible confirmed scores: a winner receives the agreed pool before any disclosed configured demo-only fee; a draw refunds each player's own stake. A cancellation releases both reservations. A settlement validates state and both participants, is atomic/idempotent and records immutable audit information.
- [ ] One missing submission preserves the other's valid evidence, enters timeout review and permits forfeit only when published rules/evidence support it, with reason recorded. Neither submits: do not award an automatic win; review or cancel/refund under documented rules. Conflicting screenshots freeze settlement, enter UNDER_REVIEW and notify participants; never blindly transfer credits on deadline.

## Disputes, administration, notifications, and history — in progress
- [ ] Players can open a dispute for conflicting scores, missing evidence, incorrect screenshot, disconnect/abandonment, wrong opponent/settings, suspected cheating or other; include evidence attachments and explanation, status, timestamp, admin notes, reasoned resolution and resolution time.
- [ ] Use OPEN, INVESTIGATING, RESOLVED and REJECTED dispute statuses. Require administrator reason. RESOLVED and REJECTED are terminal. An authorized rejection closes the reviewed match by refunding each player's own stake; it never awards a win. Serialize status changes with resolution under database row locks. Keep audit trail for decisions and state changes; admins may not silently overwrite scores or erase transactions.
- [ ] Persist in-app notifications for challenge received/accepted/rejected, stake reserved, match ready/opponent ready/started, approaching evidence deadline, evidence submitted, result confirmed, draw refund, review/dispute updates and cancellation. Realtime can be used where configured; do not promise push notifications without a push provider.
- [ ] Provide match history, details and results, dispute views, admin activity, reported-match/conflicting-evidence review, demo ledger review, account suspension/reinstatement where authorized, and audit log access under server-side role protection.
- [ ] Let authorized administrators pause new challenge creation, set a minimum and maximum demo stake only within 10–5,000 credits, set a default match duration within 6–30 minutes, and post an optional short player-facing operational notice. Validate configuration server-side, require a reason, audit changes, and apply them only to new challenges; never create monetary settings or change existing challenge/match terms.

## Persistence, security, and deployment — in progress
- [ ] Keep version-controlled migrations for profiles, preferences, challenges, matches, participants, match codes, evidence metadata, wallet accounts/transactions, disputes, notifications and admin audit logs; use primary/foreign keys, unique/check constraints, indexes, timestamps, referential integrity and database functions/transactions where atomic operations require them.
- [ ] Apply authorization to every relevant server read/write and private evidence object; no secrets in frontend; use server-side authorization, strict validation, output escaping where relevant, sensitive-operation rate limits, secure sessions, idempotency, atomic balance and state operations, upload restrictions, duplicate acceptance prevention, protection against balance manipulation/unauthorized evidence/state changes/self-awarded wins, and auditable admin operations. The selected managed MySQL service has no native application Row-Level Security; enforce ownership through server-only, relationship-scoped queries and role procedures rather than claiming database-native RLS.
- [ ] Supply `.env.example` with placeholders only; document actual database/migration, authentication callback/redirect, mail, private-storage, production-build, frontend-hosting, route-fallback, backup/recovery, monitoring/error reporting and deployment setup. Do not commit credentials.
- [ ] Keep the responsive frontend suitable for future Capacitor/React Native conversion without unnecessary mobile-app complexity.
- [ ] Do not describe the product as licensed/compliant. Before any Kenyan real-money feature, require legal review of gambling classification/licensing and selected payment-provider approval.
